## Design notes worth keeping
- The guard is stronger than asked: besides checking each hop before
  connecting, `pinned-fetch.ts` runs the address check inside the socket's own
  DNS lookup, so a hostname cannot answer "public" to the check and "private"
  to the connection (DNS rebinding).
- Direct attempt capped at 10 s per page so the Firecrawl fallback still fits
  a row's 45 s. 404 and 410 are final (Firecrawl is not asked).
- Thin page: under 200 characters of text and no mailto, tel or social link.
- Drain concurrency 6 through a page source (hard ceiling 8), at most 2
  requests in flight per host. Cron link limit 40 → 300.
