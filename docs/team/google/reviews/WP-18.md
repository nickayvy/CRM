## Findings carried forward (not blocking)
1. **PO-box zips double the cost of a city search.** `resolveArea("Dallas,
   TX")` returns 120 zips but only 60 distinct centres (54 share the city
   centroid); Austin 80 → 52, Boise 32 → 14, Tampa 59 → 32. Searches for those
   zips repeat the same query at the same point. Fix assigned to WP-20:
   collapse zips that share a centre when the area is a city or a radius.
2. **Speed in production is unmeasured.** Each place costs about a dozen
   sequential queries (upsert, snapshot, context, qualification). The runner
   is correct at any speed because of the lease and the cursor, but slices may
   do few searches each. Measure after deploy.
3. A database error inside a slice marks the job `failed`, which cannot be
   continued. WP-20 offers "Run again" on failed jobs as well as completed.
4. Phone matching compares digit strings. Google numbers carry the country
   code (11 digits); a Trustpilot phone scraped as 10 digits will not match.
   The website-domain match covers Trustpilot rows; left as is.
